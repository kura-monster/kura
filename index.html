— Community Rules —

A few simple things we ask of everyone in our space.

  1. Be respectful within the community. Internal conflict weakens everyone.
  2. No harassment or personal attacks between members.
  3. Keep personal information private — yours and others'.
  4. Follow the directions of the staff. Final decisions rest with the owner.
  5. Use the right channels for the right topics.
  6. What happens in the server stays in the server.
  7. If you see a problem, report it to staff — don't escalate it.
