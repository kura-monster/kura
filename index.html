— Community Rules —

A few simple things we ask of everyone in our space.

  1. Be respectful. Disagreement is fine; disrespect is not.
  2. No harassment, hate speech, or targeted attacks of any kind.
  3. Keep personal information private — yours and others'.
  4. No spam, scams, or unsolicited self-promotion.
  5. Use the right channels for the right topics.
  6. Listen to the staff. Final decisions rest with the owner.
  7. If you see a problem, report it — don't escalate it.
